## Analysis and visualisation of ChIP-seq and RNA-seq data

R scripts used for the analysis and visualisation of sequencing data presented in Chapter 4

| Script | Description | Figure number in thesis
|---|---|---|
| 01-chip-seq-heatmaps | HIF-1a ChIP-seq peak intensity heatmaps | Figure 4.8 and 4.10, panel A
| 02-chip-seq-volcano-plots | Differential HIF-1a binding between hypoxia vs normoxia | Figure 4.8 and 4.10, panel C
| 03-chip-seq-GO-BP-KEGG | Dot plots of enriched GO BP and KEGG of HIF-1a peaks | Figure 4.9 and 4.11, panels B and C
| 04-chip-seq-genomic-tracks | Visualisation of genomic alignment tracks | Figure 4.12, panels B-D
| 05-rna-seq-volcano-plots | Differential expression analysis in hypoxic samples in HMGN1 KD vs Ctrl | Figure 4.13
| 06-rna-seq-heatmaps | Differential expression analysis in hypoxic samples in HMGN1 KD vs Ctrl | Figure 4.14 and 4.15

Scripts were written and executed using R version 4.5.1 (2025-06-13 ucrt)
